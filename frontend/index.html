Why it stays

A warung should not need a back office to keep the day straight. One till remembers the bill, the shelf and the kitchen.

On the floor

A counter that stays open

Ring nasi goreng, es teh and the extra telur without waiting on a cloud. The bill is saved on this computer first.

Stock that matches the shelf

When the last portion of ayam goes out, the count moves with it. Low stock shows up before the lunch queue does.

A ticket the kitchen can read

Tables and takeaway print a short ticket. The guest gets a bill. Closing the day is one screen, not a notebook.
